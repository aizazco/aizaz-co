About

Strategist and practitioner

Aizaz Sheikh has spent more than fifteen years in marketing, most of it in travel, leading brand, content and community at G Adventures, Travelzoo and TourRadar. A Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Marketing, he now advises travel brands on how people discover, decide and connect.

He sits on the board of Tourism Cares, co-hosts the Travel Trends Podcast and has keynoted the Adventure Travel World Summit in Chile and Québec City. Outside travel, he's deep in football supporter culture, which is why the keynotes usually come with a jersey.
